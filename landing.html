About VOLGAN

VOLGAN is a free and open source social networking platform built to help people connect, share moments and discover communities. Our mission is to make social networking more open, accessible and community-driven. VOLGAN encourages people to communicate freely while giving developers the opportunity to explore, contribute and improve the platform. VOLGAN – Connect Freely. Share Openly.
