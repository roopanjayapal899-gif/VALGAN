Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

We believe your personal information belongs to you. This policy explains how VOLGAN handles your information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

VOLGAN may collect the following information when you use the platform:

  • Username and profile information.
  • Email address and account details.
  • Posts, comments and uploaded images.
  • Friend connections and interactions.
  • Device and technical information.

2. Location Information

If you enable location services, VOLGAN may use your location to provide nearby discovery features. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve VOLGAN.
  • To display your profile and posts.
  • To provide messaging and social features.
  • To protect users and prevent misuse.
  • To respond to support requests.

4. Information Sharing

Information may be visible to other users depending on your privacy settings and the visibility of your posts. We may also use service providers to operate the platform. We do not claim that all information is private or that sharing never occurs.

5. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable security measures to protect user information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes, subject to applicable law.

7. Your Choices

  • Update your profile information.
  • Manage account privacy settings.
  • Control location permissions.
  • Block or report other users.
  • Request account deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

VOLGAN is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. We will take appropriate steps if we discover that an account has been created in violation of these requirements.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform develops. Changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For questions or requests concerning your personal information, contact VOLGAN Support.
